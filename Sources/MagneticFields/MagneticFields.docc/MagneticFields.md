# ``MagneticFields``

Pluggable magnetic-field models with explicit coordinates, units and coefficient coverage.

Use ideal dipoles or Earth WMM/IGRF harmonics. Field vectors use tesla and body-fixed
Cartesian coordinates; geographic convenience queries use WGS84 ellipsoid heights.
