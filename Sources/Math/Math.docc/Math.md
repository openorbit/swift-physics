# ``Math``

Numerical and geometric primitives shared by the physics modules.

Math provides right-handed coordinates, rotations, matrices and frame identity.
Astronomical epochs and time-dependent frame transformations live in CelMek.

## Topics

### Rotations and frames
- ``Quaternion``
- ``Matrix3x3``
- ``Frame``
- ``Transformation``

### Angles and coordinates
- ``HourAngle``
- ``AngleOfArc``
- ``GeographicCoordinate``
- ``EquatorialCoordinate``
- ``EclipticCoordinate``
