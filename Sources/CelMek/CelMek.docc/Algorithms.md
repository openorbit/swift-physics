# Astronomical algorithms and references

The original CelMek library contains VSOP87 planetary series, ELP2000-82b lunar
series and a Goffin Pluto implementation.

The public provider contracts, native frames, time scales, SI conversion and
coverage are described in <doc:EPHEMERIDES>. Approximation policies and measured
error are described in <doc:ACCURACY> and <doc:VSOP87_ACCURACY_CHARACTERIZATION>.

## Original bibliography

The retired Antora documentation cited the following works. Its BibTeX entries
are preserved in `Documentation/bibliography.bib` in the repository.

- Jean Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms*, second edition, Willmann-Bell, 1998,
  ISBN 0-943396-61-1.
- E. Goffin, J. Meeus and C. Steyaert, *An accurate representation of the motion
  of Pluto*, Astronomy and Astrophysics 155(2), 323–325, 1986.
